What We Automate

One AI agent, every kind of call your business makes.

Every workflow below runs on the same AI engine, trained on your business, policies and tone — so it sounds like you, not a script reader. Whether you're booking, collecting, selling, supporting or hiring, each workflow is live in days, logs every conversation, and hands off to a human the moment a call needs one.

TypeChannelWhat it does
Order & Booking Confirmation
Voice
Calls every order, booking or reservation before it's fulfilled to verify the customer and the details — including cash-on-delivery orders for e-commerce. Fake or unintentional bookings get flagged and pulled before you commit resources to them.
Delivery & RTO Recovery
Voice/WhatsApp
Reaches the customer while an order or shipment is in transit — confirms the window, fixes a wrong address, or reschedules — so it doesn't come back undelivered. Follow-up runs on WhatsApp if the call doesn't connect.
Sales & Lead Qualification
Voice/WhatsApp
Calls new leads within minutes of sign-up or enquiry, asks the qualifying questions your sales team would, and books a follow-up slot on the spot. Cold leads get a WhatsApp nudge instead of going cold in a spreadsheet.
Payments & Collections
Voice/WhatsApp
Calls customers ahead of a due EMI, invoice, rent or subscription renewal, and drops a WhatsApp message with a direct payment link right after — so reminders convert instead of getting ignored.
Appointment & Scheduling
Voice/WhatsApp
Confirms upcoming appointments, consultations, site visits or service slots a day in advance, and offers to reschedule on the same call — useful for clinics, salons, real estate, and any business that runs on bookings.
Recruitment & Interview Scheduling
Voice/WhatsApp
Calls shortlisted candidates, screens them against your basic criteria, and books interview slots directly onto your team's calendar — so HR isn't chasing callbacks for every open role.
Customer Support & Front Desk
Voice
Answers order status, account, and FAQ-type calls the moment they come in, any hour of the day — acting as a virtual receptionist for businesses that can't staff a 24/7 line. Anything outside the script gets escalated to your team with full call context.
Feedback & Surveys
Voice/WhatsApp
Calls or messages the customer right after a purchase, visit or service completion to capture a rating and a reason behind it, while it's still fresh — not a generic email survey opened three weeks later.
Analytics & Reporting
Dashboard
Every call and message rolls up into one dashboard — connect rate, outcome, recovery saved, leads converted, revenue impact — broken down by campaign, channel and day, so you know exactly what's working.
