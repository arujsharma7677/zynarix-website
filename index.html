# Zynarix > Zynarix is an AI calling and WhatsApp automation company serving businesses in India. Its AI voice agents make and answer business phone calls 24/7 — order confirmation, delivery and RTO recovery, lead qualification, payment reminders, appointment scheduling, recruitment screening, customer support and feedback — with WhatsApp follow-up and analytics on every conversation. Contact: sales@zynarix.in Website: https://zynarix.in/ Area served: India Pricing: quoted on call volume and workflows; 7-day free trial, no payment if not satisfied Setup time: most workflows live within a few days, no code required Languages: English, Hindi and other major Indian languages, switchable mid-call ## What an AI calling agent is An AI calling agent is software that holds a real spoken conversation on a phone call instead of a human agent. It listens, understands the customer, replies in a natural voice, and completes a task. Unlike an IVR, the customer never presses a key or navigates a menu. ## Services - **Order & booking confirmation calls**: verifies orders, bookings and cash-on-delivery orders before fulfilment; flags fake or accidental orders. - **Delivery & RTO recovery**: calls customers while a shipment is in transit to confirm the window, fix addresses or reschedule; WhatsApp follow-up if the call doesn't connect. - **AI sales calling & lead qualification**: calls new leads within minutes, qualifies them, books follow-up slots. - **Payment reminders & collections**: calls before EMI, invoice, rent or renewal due dates and sends a WhatsApp payment link. - **Appointment confirmation & scheduling**: confirms appointments a day ahead and reschedules on the same call. - **Recruitment screening & interview scheduling**: screens shortlisted candidates and books interview slots on the team's calendar. - **AI customer support & virtual receptionist**: answers inbound order status, account and FAQ calls 24/7; escalates with full context. - **Feedback & survey calls**: captures ratings and reasons right after a purchase, visit or service. - **Call analytics & reporting**: recordings, transcripts, outcome scoring, campaign-level dashboards and real-time alerts. ## How it works 1. Connect your order, lead, candidate or customer list — upload, API or CRM sync. 2. AI agents call and WhatsApp on your schedule, in your customer's language. 3. The agent handles the conversation live and escalates to a human when needed. 4. Every outcome lands in a dashboard with the recording and transcript attached. ## Industries served E-commerce and D2C, retail, BFSI, healthcare and clinics, logistics, real estate, education, recruitment and HR, insurance, hospitality and travel, automotive, legal services, SaaS and tech, local services. ## Pages - [Homepage](https://zynarix.in/): overview, services, analytics, comparison with IVR and call centres, FAQ. - [FAQ](https://zynarix.in/#faq): cost, languages, setup time, CRM integration, escalation, recordings. - [AI calling vs IVR vs call centre](https://zynarix.in/#compare): side-by-side comparison.
