What is an AI calling agent?

An AI calling agent is software that holds a real spoken conversation on a phone call instead of a human agent. It listens, understands what the customer says, replies in a natural voice, and completes a task — confirming an order, qualifying a lead, reminding about a payment, booking an appointment or answering a support question. Unlike an IVR, the customer never presses a key or navigates a menu. They just talk.

Zynarix is an AI calling and WhatsApp automation company that builds these agents for businesses in India. The agent is trained on your business, policies and tone, runs outbound and inbound calls 24/7, follows up on WhatsApp when a call isn't answered, escalates to a human when a conversation needs one, and records every outcome in a dashboard you can act on.

Frequently Asked Questions

AI calling, answered.

The questions businesses ask us before they run their first campaign.

How does Zynarix AI calling software work?

You connect your customer, order, lead or candidate list. Zynarix trains an AI agent on your business, policies and tone, then calls or WhatsApps on your schedule. The agent handles the conversation live, completes the task or escalates to your team with full context, and writes every outcome to a dashboard with a recording and transcript attached. Most workflows are live within a few days and need no code on your side.

What languages can the AI calling agent speak?

Zynarix agents speak to customers in their own language — English, Hindi and other major Indian languages — and can switch mid-call when a customer replies in a different one. Scripts and WhatsApp templates are set up per language for each campaign.

How is an AI calling agent different from an IVR or a call centre?

An IVR plays a menu and asks the caller to press keys; it can't handle anything outside that menu. A human call centre handles open conversation but costs per seat, works fixed shifts, and can't scale to thousands of calls in an hour. An AI calling agent holds an open conversation like a human, runs 24/7, starts thousands of calls at once, and logs and scores every call automatically — while still escalating anything it shouldn't finish alone.

How much does AI calling cost, and is there a free trial?

Pricing depends on your call volume, the channels you use, and the workflows you run, so we quote it after a short discovery call. Every new business starts with a 7-day free trial — if you're not satisfied during the trial, you don't pay anything.

How quickly can we go live?

Most workflows go live within a few days. Setup is: share your call flow and policies, connect your list or CRM, approve the script and WhatsApp templates, then run a test batch before the full campaign opens up.

Does Zynarix integrate with our CRM or order system?

Yes. Zynarix can pull lists from and write outcomes back to common CRMs, order management systems, spreadsheets and internal tools via file upload, API or webhooks — so call outcomes show up where your team already works, not in a separate tab nobody opens.

What happens if the AI can't answer a customer's question?

It escalates. Anything outside its scope is transferred or handed to your team with the full transcript, the customer's details, and what was already discussed — so the human doesn't start from zero. Escalation rate shows up in the dashboard, so you can see exactly where the script needs work.

Are calls recorded, and can we review them later?

Every call is recorded and transcribed automatically, and every WhatsApp message is logged. Conversations are searchable by keyword, outcome or customer, so a disputed order or a missed commitment can be checked directly instead of reconstructed from memory.

Can AI agents handle inbound calls as well as outbound?

Both. Outbound, the agent runs your campaigns — confirmations, reminders, qualification, screening. Inbound, it acts as a 24/7 virtual receptionist: answering order status, account and FAQ calls the moment they land, at any hour, and routing anything it shouldn't handle to your team.

Which industries use AI calling agents?

E-commerce and D2C brands, retail, BFSI and insurance, healthcare and clinics, logistics, real estate, education, recruitment and HR teams, hospitality and travel, automotive, legal services, SaaS companies and local service businesses. Any business that calls customers or receives calls can automate part of that volume.
